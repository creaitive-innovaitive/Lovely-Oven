Where we began

Our Story

I learned to cook the traditional way: clean, fresh food, prepared by family for family. When I moved to Hanoi for university, I began to develop a style of my own, and as I met more foreigners I adapted the ingredients and flavours to suit them, without losing the care I grew up with. After a few meals for people at local workplaces, the idea grew quickly into what we are today. Now we cook for individuals and groups, locally and city-wide, as ready-to-eat meals and bulk orders.
