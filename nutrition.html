Our Process

How We Cook

Every batch starts the same way. A base marinade, a hot oven, and a finish that's added only when it's needed. No deep frying, no shortcuts.

🧪

The Base Marinade

All chicken is marinated overnight (4–12 hours) in olive oil, minced garlic, paprika, garlic powder, salt, pepper, and lemon juice or vinegar. This forms the flavour foundation every dish is built on.

🌡️

Oven Roasted

Cooked at 180–200°C for 18–22 minutes. No deep fryers, no reheating from frozen — just steady, even oven heat that locks in moisture and builds a proper crust. Sauces and glazes are brushed on in the final 5 minutes.

⚡

Ready in 8–10 Minutes

Batches are pre-marinated and 70–80% roasted in advance. When you order, your portion is finished in 5–7 minutes with its sauce. Fresh flavour, fast delivery — no compromise.

Where It Comes From

Our Ingredients & Sourcing

We keep the ingredient list short and honest. Fresh chicken, real herbs and spices, and quality sauces — sourced as locally as we can.

🐓

Free-Range Chicken

We use boneless chicken thighs — a naturally juicier cut that stays tender through oven roasting. Sourced fresh daily, never frozen. Thighs also offer a better protein-to-fat ratio than processed alternatives.

🌿

Herbs & Spices

Garlic, paprika, rosemary, thyme, chilli, lemon and lime — whole, fresh, and measured by hand. No flavour powders, no MSG, no artificial enhancers. The marinade is made fresh for each batch.

🫙

Sauces & Oils

Olive oil is our base fat — no seed oils. Sauces (BBQ, mustard, honey, mayonnaise) are quality branded products chosen for flavour and clean ingredient lists. We always check labels for allergen changes between batches.
